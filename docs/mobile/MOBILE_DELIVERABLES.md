## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do we need to develop mobile apps?

**No.** Use official Bitwarden apps (iOS & Android). They work perfectly with Vaultwarden.

### How much does it cost?

**$0.** Apps are free to download and use.

### How long does setup take?

**5 minutes** per user. Just download, configure server URL, and login.

### Will auto-fill work?

**Yes.** Full system-integrated auto-fill on both iOS and Android.

### Do we need custom apps for branding?

**No,** unless you're a massive enterprise (50,000+ users) with unlimited budget. For 99.9% of deployments, official apps are the best choice.

### What about security?

Official Bitwarden apps are:
- Security-audited
- Open source
- Battle-tested (millions of users)
- Regularly updated

### Can we add custom features?

If you really need custom features, it costs $400k+ and takes 6-12 months. For most cases, the official apps have all needed features.

### More questions?

See [FAQ](../extensions/FAQ.md) or [Mobile Setup Guide](MOBILE_SETUP_GUIDE.md#troubleshooting)
